<div class="wrap section center">
  <h1>Connecting the world with art</h1>
  <div class="prose-center">
    <p>Beautify Earth partners with local artists to connect the world with art through murals, street art, and technological resources. For over 15 years, the organization has committed to making art accessible to all through street art productions worldwide.</p>
    <p>The mission centers on providing creative strategies and technological resources that advocate for art, artists, and vibrant communities. The approach integrates a regenerative framework considering positive social and environmental impacts while increasing awareness, loyalty, and profit.</p>
    <p>The team comprises artists, technologists, and urban beautification experts available for consultation.</p>
    <p><a href="https://www.beautifyearth.com/contact-us">Contact us</a> to learn more and discuss your ideas and plans. <a href="https://art.beautifyearth.com/signup">Sign up</a> on our platform to join the thousands of artists, landlords, brand managers and city officials who've used Beautify to make urban beauty a reality.</p>
  </div>
