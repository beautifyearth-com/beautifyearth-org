  <h2 class="centerhead">What a mural campaign costs</h2>
  <p class="lead">Three parts, each priced on its own. The figures come from the campaigns we have run.</p>
  <div class="cards">
    <div class="card boxed">
      <h3>Wall rental</h3>
      <p>$1,000 to $50,000 a month, depending on the wall's size, visibility and traffic. Most campaigns run one to three months.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card boxed">
      <h3>Artist and production</h3>
      <p>Muralists typically charge $10 to $50 per square foot, plus paint and lift. Detail and height move the number.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card boxed">
      <h3>Management</h3>
      <p>Beautify sources the wall, manages the artist and the production, and documents the finished work for your channels. Or run the project yourself on the platform.</p>
