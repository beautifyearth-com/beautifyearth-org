# Every School Should Be Beautiful

Our programs make it easy to Beautify your school. Certified Charity 501(c)(3)

## Every School
Beautify Earth develops mural and street art programs in partnership with cities, NGOs, schools, and mission-aligned businesses to engage local schools and students in beautification of their facilities, hallways, schoolyards, and classrooms.

- [Sponsor a School](https://www.beautifyearth.com/contact)
- [Beautify My School](https://beautifyearth.com/contact)
