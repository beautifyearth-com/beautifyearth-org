# Mural Agreement & Project Terms

#### Definitions

Platform: BeautifyEarth.com and any service rendered on this domain or its URL variants.

Premise(s): the location where the wall or project is located.

Start Date: the date mural begin as agreed by both parties through the platform

Completion Date: the date mural be completed as agreed by both parties through the platform Project: the mural, painting project or other artist service provided through the Beautify platform Artwork: The design intended to be executed at a specific Premise(s). Artwork is to be considered the platform "approved" artwork as noted in any communication facilitated by the platform, or directly through the platform during the Design Phase, where both Artist and Wall Owner agree to move forward in writing with said Artwork.

Materials: supplies needed for Artist to create Artwork that include but are not limited to paint, ladders, scaffolding, lift rentals, lighting, brushes, rollers, chalk, buckets, paint trays, extension poles, rags, tape and plastic drop cloths.

Artist: any person or entity that is directly or indirectly engaged, in some capacity, in the creation of a mural, provided, however, that any rights attributed to an Artist herein shall only apply to an Artist in the event that such person or entity compliantly enters into a Project utilizing the Platform, as further described herein.

Wall Owner: Any person or entity that directly or indirectly owns or has the ability to engage in a Project on behalf of a rights holder with respect to a Premises (i.e. express authorization from the building’s owner to modify the building according to the terms of this Agreement), provided, however, that any rights attributed to Wall Owner or Wall Owner herein shall only apply in the event that such person or entity compliantly enters into a Project utilizing the Platform, as further described herein.

Project Owner: Any person or entity that is paying for Beautify platform, wall, artist, or other services to execute projects.

BE/Beautify: Beautify Earth, Inc. is a for-profit corporation with, a 501(c)3 non-profit arm, both with the mission to make positive social and economic impacts in communities through art initiatives.

Parties: BE, Artist, Wall Owner, or Sponsor, referred to collectively.

## Project Stages & Services

#### Design Fees

####

- Beautify collects a "design fee" as a deposit to ensure timeliness and respect for all parties.

- Once a design fee is paid, or the project commences through the platform, the Parties agree to communicate in a timely fashion in order to be respectful of one another's time, and to keep the project moving forward according to one another's expectations.

#### Art / Content & Community Guidelines

For outdoor murals:

- all Parties agree not to partake in product or service advertising through the platform. BE encourages brand and community sponsors with included attribution on the work, and artwork focused on aligned values between all parties, but Artwork must be primarily created with the intention of benefiting the community, neighborhood or street at large. The design should strive to have a positive and/or affirming design and/or message with the intention to uplift and inspire.

- no logos, or brand or product names within the actual art.

- do leverage brand mission, purpose, values, colors, and tasteful attribution with @artistName and #beautifyearth.

- general rule: "if it looks like an ad, it's an ad."

#### Project Completion

- In cooperation with all Parties, Artist shall provide services to create a certain Artwork at the Premises. Artist will work with the Parties to select themes, concepts and ideas for the Artwork if needed, OR, Artist will design and complete artwork based on the design approved by the one or more Parties.

- Artist’s services shall be performed in a professional manner consistent with Artist’s prior work, and Artist represents and warrants that Design and Artwork is Artist’s original creation.

- Wall Owner will provide access to the site, as well as power, water, restrooms (as needed) in order for the Artist to complete the Artwork in a timely manner.

#### Start & Completion Dates

####

- The Artist shall undertake the creation of the Artwork on or after the Start Date, or date agreed upon with the Parties.

- The Artist shall complete the Artwork on or by the Completion Date, or date agreed upon with the Parties.

#### Materials

####

- Artist shall purchase and/or rent Materials necessary for the creation of their Artwork. Any logistics to complete mural are the the responsibility of the artist. Site visits and a clear understanding of equipment needed are necessary for the artist be fiscally responsible and save time. Beautify will not reimburse for incorrect equipment ordered by artist.

- Artist acknowledges that BE may have access to discounted Materials.

#### Project Closure

####

Project is defined as closed when:

- Artwork has a substantial look, feel and design as specified in the "Design Phase." Parties understand that the Artwork will retain the general design proposed by the Artist, but may have minor differences for artistic purposes, or obstruction on the wall that prevent identical replication (e.g. windows, pipes, doors, electrical boxes, etc.).

- Artist signs Artwork if they choose.

- Artist includes “#beautifyearth” and any hashtags requested by Parties near Artist’s signature on the Artwork.

- Parties include information about Artwork creation and purpose, including acknowledgement of corporate sponsors, to be located near the Artwork (“Attribution”), with size, location and specifics of the Attribution to be determined by the Parties.

- Parties include acknowledgment to Beautify Earth and Artist in any public relations stories or events in connection with the Artwork including all relevant social media, including Facebook and Instagram, and promotional materials, by mentioning and tagging “Beautify Earth”, “@beautifyearth” and “#beautifyearth”.

- Artist submits a minimum of one high resolution digital photograph of the completed Artwork from a wide angle (to show entire mural on wall) to their profile. Artist submits any additional photographs of Artwork, including before and after photos from the same angle.

## Art Preservation & Maintenance of the Artwork

####

#### Wall Owner acknowledgements

- Wall Owner shall make reasonable efforts to notify BE promptly in the event of the need for any maintenance or restoration services so that the Artist may have a reasonable opportunity to perform such work him/herself or to supervise or consult in its performance.

- Wall Owner shall take reasonable precautions to protect Artwork against damage or destruction by external forces, and to preserve the integrity of the wall. All parties understand that walls with full access to the street make full protection of the art hard to guarantee."

- If the Wall Owner becomes aware of a sale of the building or intention by anyone to remove the Artwork, Wall Owner shall notify BE at least sixty (60) days before such event to allow reasonable time for the Artist to make the desired modifications if necessary in order to document and/or try to remove their artwork without damaging the property if applicable.

- Wall Owner shall notify BE if Artwork is damaged, including graffiti as soon as possible.

- Wall Owner acknowledges that Sponsor has the ability to host activities to promote mural, including but not limited to having media, local community members and corporate partners visit mural.

#### Artist acknowledgements

Artist acknowledges that:

- the duration of the Artwork may be beyond the control of non-artist Parties.

- the outdoor environment is constantly changing and the Artwork may not comply with a future vision of the area.

All art is subject to normal wear and tear and may change based on the environment in which it exists.

## Delay

####

#### Disability

Artist shall immediately notify BE of a delay of the execution of the Artworresulting from an incapacitating, illness, or injury. If such an event prevents Artist from completing the Artwork according to schedule, Sponsor may request a different artist for the Project and will pay Artist a reasonable compensation for work completed.

####

#### Unavoidable Occurrences

If Artist is willing and able but cannot perform any of their respective obligations due to events beyond Artist’s control, Sponsor shall have the right to extend by a period of time equal to the duration of such events, terminate the Project and/or replace it with a different Artist at a different location suitable to Sponsor’s Schedule set forth in the Appendix, or any combination of the above with written notification to BE, and by mutually agreed upon resolutions and/or compensations for fairness if needed. Events beyond Artist's control include, but are not limited to, acts of God, war, civil commotion, labor disputes, strikes, fire, flood or other casualty, shortages of labor or material, government regulation or restriction and weather conditions.
