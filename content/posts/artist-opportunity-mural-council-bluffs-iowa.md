# New mural matchup opportunity in Council Bluffs, IA

Beautify is thrilled to announce a Mural Matchup open for proposals in Council Bluffs, IA!

## About the Project:

**Approx. Dimensions:** 110' W x 25' H

**Suggested Budget:** $18,000

**Artist Guidance:** "We are looking to feature representational designs which interpret the agricultural history of western Iowa, or showcase the Missouri River and/or Loess Hills - the two most salient physical features of the area."

## Proposal Details:

Please submit a proposal that includes the following information through the [wall profile page](https://marketplace.beautifyearth.com/#/walls/728):

1. **Vision**: A written description of your proposed design that includes potential colors and content.

2. **Imagery**: An image of your design concept. Multiple images should be compiled into a single PDF.

3. **Budget**: A proposed budget that includes materials, labor, equipment, and Beautify's 22% service fee. Please review the suggested budget but feel free to submit a budget you feel is appropriate.

[**Submit Proposal**](https://marketplace.beautifyearth.com/#/walls/728)

Once your proposal is submitted, wall owners will be able to directly message you in the proposal. Make sure to check your [dashboard](https://marketplace.beautifyearth.com/#/dashboard) for updates! When a final artist is selected, a notification will be sent to all artists. Please contact moral@beautifyearth.com with any questions about the project or proposal requirements.

If you have previously submitted a proposal and are awaiting a response, we thank you for your patience as we work to push projects forward and navigate through this challenging time. We are still waiting to finalize artists on a number of projects and will notify the artist community once projects are closed. If you have any questions or would like an update on a specific project please chat with us on our site to speak directly to a Beautify team member.

Learn more about becoming a part of the Beautify artist community [here](https://www.beautifyearth.com/artists).
