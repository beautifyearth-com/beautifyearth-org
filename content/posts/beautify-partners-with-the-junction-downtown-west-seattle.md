The Junction is Downtown West Seattle--the West Seattle neighborhood's main street and center stage.

It's the hub of activity where the West Seattle community connects for coffee, shopping, business activities, doctor visits, dinner, drinks and camaraderie.

Beautify is proud to work with the Junction to deliver a new level of experience for it's residents, tourists, businesses, and city dwellers. We are excited to help bring more beauty to the streets of Seattle with the help of The Junction, bringing about a brighter and more artful community through the power of murals and local community.
