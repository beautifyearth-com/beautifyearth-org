**Author:** Beautify Team

**Date:** Oct 12, 2020 | **Read time:** 3 min read

At Beautify Earth, we believe in fostering community-driven transformation. This initiative centers on the transformative and rejuvenating power of butterflies, launched by Daniela Mattus, Director of Education, at Lincoln Middle School in Santa Monica.

The project partnered the Santa Monica Police Department with artists Cesar Duran and Corie Mattie. Sgt Scott McGee coordinated officer participation — notably, he attended the school as a student.

**Daniela's account:**

During the second month of COVID lockdown, Daniela observed Santa Monica's empty streets. While driving, she spotted butterflies repeatedly, which she interpreted as "little reminders to go beyond my fear and to be present." This inspired the butterfly initiative as a "symbol of resilience and resurgence" and healing markers for the community.

The first implementation occurred at Lincoln Middle School (15th and California). Officers spent hours stenciling butterflies on courtyard walls. Sgt. Gerardo Leyva reflected: "I'm not an artist but I'm humbled to have been a part of such an experience that spreads positivity."

Sgt Scott McGee added: "Serving the community that I grew up in is a great honor... Being able to bring a lightness, beauty and a symbol of transformation through these butterflies is a positive step."

The organization seeks to "Butterfly" Santa Monica and expand citywide. Community support is welcomed through [donations](/nonprofit/) or [school mural sign-ups](https://marketplace.beautifyearth.com/signup).
