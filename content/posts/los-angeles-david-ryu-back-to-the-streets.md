Thanks to council-member David Ryu from CD4 in Los Angeles, the [Back to the Streets program](/back-to-the-streets/) is getting a major boost. The council-member has approved a $500 match for each wall in his district funded and painted through beautifyearth.com.

The back to the streets program is helping to reignite and bring back life to main streets all over the US. It's encouraging business owners to enlist their wall to turn them into inspirational art, additional sponsors to help where needed, and engaging local community to listen to all voices. It's truly a head to toe approach to making every wall beautiful.

Murals (in order) by [Gino Flo](https://marketplace.beautifyearth.com/#/artists/ginoflo), artist [Even Mendel](https://marketplace.beautifyearth.com/#/artists/evanmendel) and [Hans Haveron](https://marketplace.beautifyearth.com/#/artists/hanshaveron)

Beautify and CD4 have a [history](/post/great-street-initiatives/) of creating effective change together through art and murals, having brought much inspiration to neighborhoods in Los Angeles. One of our most memorable projects together was the [great streets](http://lagreatstreets.org/western) project. Through our work on Western Blvd in partnership with David Ryu and Mayor Garcetti, this project produced 10 murals and several utility boxes initiating street and community revitalization.
